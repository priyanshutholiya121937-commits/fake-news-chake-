About TruthCheck AI

An end-to-end intelligence system engineered to detect sensationalist misinformation, deceptive claims, and fabricated news across text and photographic media.

1. Purpose & Core Capabilities

TruthCheck AI combines Natural Language Processing (NLP), statistical Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) feature weighting, Machine Learning classifiers, and computer vision Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to evaluate news authenticity in real-time.

4. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Engine

Our custom image pipeline (ocr/image_processor.py) optimizes camera feeds using adaptive bilateral denoising, Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE), and dynamic thresholding before feeding glyphs to EasyOCR deep learning networks.

5. Limitations & Ethical Considerations

Statistical Pattern vs Factual Ground Truth: TruthCheck AI evaluates stylistic, linguistic, syntactic, and structural markers commonly observed in deceptive versus reputable journalism.

It is designed as an assistive verification aid and should be complemented by primary source verification, institutional fact-check registries, and critical human judgment.
